## Core Thesis

Agentic AI is not a single technology — it's a convergence of RAG, reasoning models, and autonomous workflow orchestration that enables AI systems to DO WORK, not just generate text. February 2026: Anthropic announced a legal AI agent (not chatbot, not copilot) — autonomous system for legal research, contract review, regulatory analysis. SaaS stocks went into freefall.

## Six Phases of Agentic Evolution

1. RAG & Fine-Tuning — ground responses in real data
2. Reasoning Models — step-by-step problem solving (>50% of token generation now)
3. Agentic IT Automation — adaptive, goal-oriented systems
4. Multi-Agent Systems — multiple agents collaborate
5. Agentic DevOps — autonomous software delivery
6. Agentic RAG — autonomous agents that plan, iterate, self-correct across data sources

Each phase builds on previous. Capabilities are cumulative, value compounds.

## Industry-by-Industry Analysis

### Financial Services (Early Adopter)
- Credit risk assessment, fraud detection, regulatory reporting, customer onboarding
- 40-60% reduction in routine compliance task time
- 30% improvement in customer onboarding speed
- Measurable error rate reductions in document-intensive processes

### Healthcare (High Stakes, High Reward)
- Synthesizing patient records, diagnostic reasoning, care coordination, claims processing
- Enormous potential but uniquely stringent accuracy/privacy/accountability requirements
- Hallucination in diagnostic support = life-threatening (vs inconvenience in customer service)
- Will adopt more cautiously but competitive advantage for those who get it right

### Professional Services (Existential Disruption)
- Law firms, consulting, accounting — sell human expertise by the hour
- AI agents performing contract analysis, due diligence, regulatory research, document drafting
- Quality level not replacing senior attorneys but dramatically reducing need for junior associates
- Implications for law firm economics where associate labor is primary profit driver

### Manufacturing (Physical-Digital Convergence)
- Production scheduling, supply chain coordination, quality control, predictive maintenance
- Agentic AI introduces adaptability vs rigid rule-based traditional automation
- Agents adjust schedules for supply chain disruptions, reroute inspections based on real-time defect patterns

### Telecoms
- Network optimization, predictive maintenance, churn prediction, automated service provisioning
- Petabytes of underutilized data about signal quality, usage patterns, equipment performance
- 8-15% fuel cost reductions, 12-20% on-time delivery improvements (logistics parallel)

### Transportation & Logistics
- Dynamic route optimization based on real-time traffic, weather, delivery windows, fuel costs
- Clear, quantifiable ROI metrics — translates directly to bottom line

## The SaaS Disruption

Much of what SaaS charges for (managing workflows, organizing data, automating processes, providing UIs) is precisely what AI agents can now do. If an agent can manage your sales pipeline, generate invoices, draft contracts, handle customer service — why pay $50/user/month for software requiring humans to operate?

Pattern prediction: AI-native companies building from scratch will win over incumbents bolting AI onto existing products. Same pattern as mobile disruption of desktop software.

## Agent Maturity Levels

| Level | Capability | Example |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| 0 | Repetitive tasks | FAQ chatbots |
| 1 | Information retrieval | Knowledge base search |
| 2 | Simple orchestration | Meeting scheduling + follow-up |
| 3 | Complex orchestration | Sales pipeline across CRM + service + finance |
| 4 | Multi-agent orchestration | Orders + inventory + feedback across departments |

Most enterprises at Level 0-2. The investment thesis rests on rapid progression to Levels 3-4.

## Investment Landscape

- 2024: $1.8B raised across 69 agentic AI deals

- Most compelling startups: domain-specific agent systems, not better language models
- Playbook: pick large, process-heavy industry → understand workflows → automate painful/expensive parts
